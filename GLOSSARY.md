# Context Kernel retrieval

Context Kernel assembles relevant material for a bounded need and carries the source and access basis of the material returned.

## Language

**Context Need**:
A bounded request for material needed to understand or pursue a particular task.

**Context Packet**:
The cited material assembled in answer to a Context Need and accompanied by its retrieval status.

**Reference Citation**:
A source-attributed pointer to a particular piece of reference material found or resolved during retrieval.

**Context Access**:
A determination of whether the requester may retrieve material under the declared source scope.
_Avoid_: Action Grant, authority decision to execute

**Session Context**:
State retained for continuity within a working session.

**Retrieval Completeness**:
The stated extent to which a retrieval satisfied the requirements of its Context Need.

## Sources and boundary

[Context Kernel’s access and retrieval contract](README.md#global-invariants) and its [context forms](README.md#three-kinds-of-context-fail-differently) govern these terms. A Context Packet retains its citations and Retrieval Completeness when it informs a CEP Context Bundle or Cognate Working Context. Context Access governs the retrieval itself. Permission for any consequential operation must come from the corresponding action authority.
